# LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy Research

## Overview

This repository contains research and strategic analysis of LinkedIn content from Lara Acosta and Justin Welsh.

The objective of this project is to identify recurring content patterns, audience engagement techniques, storytelling frameworks, and positioning strategies that can be adapted for B2B SaaS organic growth.

* Justin Welsh

### Content Types

* YouTube Content

## Research Objectives

* Analyze high-performing LinkedIn creators
* Identify common content frameworks
* Compare educational and storytelling approaches
* Extract actionable insights for B2B SaaS marketing
* Develop a repeatable LinkedIn content playbook

---

## Key Findings

* Strong hooks are critical for capturing attention.
* Storytelling increases engagement and trust.
* Educational content strengthens authority.
* Positioning matters more than reach alone.
* Simplicity and clarity consistently outperform complexity.

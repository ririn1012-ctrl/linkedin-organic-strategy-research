# B2B SaaS LinkedIn Organic Content Playbook

## Objective

Build authority, trust, and inbound demand through consistent LinkedIn content.

This playbook is based on content patterns observed from Lara Acosta and Justin Welsh.

---

# Content Pillars

## 1. Founder Stories

Purpose:

Build credibility and human connection.

Examples:

* Lessons learned from building the company
* Mistakes made and what changed
* Personal experiences from leadership

Target Outcome:

Trust and relatability.

---

## 2. Educational Insights

Purpose:

Demonstrate expertise.

Examples:

* Industry trends
* Frameworks
* Step-by-step explanations
* Best practices

Target Outcome:

Authority and thought leadership.

---

## 3. Customer Success Stories

Purpose:

Show real-world impact.

Examples:

* Case studies
* Customer outcomes
* Before-and-after results

Target Outcome:

Social proof.

---

## 4. Industry Opinions

Purpose:

Create engagement and discussion.

Examples:

* Contrarian viewpoints
* Predictions
* Commentary on industry changes

Target Outcome:

Visibility and conversation.

---

# Recommended Content Structure

## Hook

Capture attention immediately.

Examples:

* Contrarian statement
* Surprising statistic
* Personal transformation
* Unexpected lesson

---

## Story or Context

Provide background and relevance.

---

## Insight

Explain the lesson or observation.

---

## Actionable Takeaway

Give readers something practical they can apply.

---

# Posting Guidelines

Frequency:

* 4 to 5 posts per week

Content Mix:

* 40% Educational
* 30% Storytelling
* 20% Customer Success
* 10% Industry Opinions

---

# Success Metrics

Primary Metrics:

* Engagement rate
* Profile visits
* Follower growth
* Comments

Business Metrics:

* Qualified leads
* Demo requests
* Inbound opportunities

---

# Final Recommendation

The most effective B2B SaaS LinkedIn strategy combines Lara Acosta's educational framework approach with Justin Welsh's storytelling approach.

Educational content builds authority.

Storytelling builds trust.

Together, they create a sustainable content engine that supports long-term audience growth and business outcomes.

# Justin Welsh LinkedIn Post 03

## Main Topic

Ignoring criticism and focusing on measurable results.

## Hook

Many people will criticize your decisions despite having no experience producing similar results.

## Content Summary

Justin explains that opinions are abundant, but results are rare.

He encourages creators and entrepreneurs to evaluate advice based on evidence rather than popularity.

The post emphasizes making decisions based on data, experience, and outcomes.

## Content Structure

### 1. Strong Opinion

The post opens with a bold statement.

### 2. Common Problem

Readers are reminded how often they receive unsolicited advice.

### 3. Alternative Approach

Justin recommends focusing on proven results and real-world evidence.

### 4. Takeaway

Success comes from testing, learning, and measuring outcomes.

## Why It Works

* Creates strong engagement
* Encourages discussion
* Reinforces authority
* Appeals to ambitious professionals

## Marketing Purpose

The post positions Justin as an independent thinker who prioritizes evidence over consensus.

This strengthens trust among entrepreneurs and creators seeking practical guidance.

## My Observation

Justin frequently uses opinion-based content to reinforce his personal brand. His strongest posts combine a bold perspective with practical lessons drawn from real-world experience.

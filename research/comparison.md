# Creator Comparison: Lara Acosta vs Justin Welsh

## Overview

Both Lara Acosta and Justin Welsh have built strong LinkedIn brands, but they use different content approaches to achieve audience growth and authority.

---

## Content Style

### Lara Acosta

Primary style:

* Educational
* Framework-driven
* Tactical
* Growth-focused

Content often teaches specific methods, systems, and LinkedIn strategies.

### Justin Welsh

Primary style:

* Story-driven
* Experience-based
* Reflective
* Lifestyle-oriented

Content often focuses on lessons learned through entrepreneurship and personal growth.

---

## Hook Strategy

### Lara Acosta

Common hooks:

* Contrarian insights
* Framework introductions
* Positioning challenges

Example approach:

"It was never a content problem. It was a positioning problem."

### Justin Welsh

Common hooks:

* Personal transformations
* Bold opinions
* Unexpected outcomes

Example approach:

"I left a $550,000 job at age 38."

### Justin Welsh

## Key Takeaway

Lara Acosta demonstrates how educational frameworks can build authority, while Justin Welsh demonstrates how storytelling can create trust and emotional connection.

The strongest LinkedIn strategy combines elements from both approaches:

* Strong storytelling
* Practical education
* Clear positioning
* Consistent authority building
